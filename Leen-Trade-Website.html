About the Company

A trusted partner in import & export.

Leen Trade Co. specializes in the import and export of high-quality food and plastic products between Lebanon and a selection of reliable international markets. From our headquarters in Saida, we operate across four core countries with established logistics corridors by sea and land.

Our mission is to deliver excellence through quality products, competitive prices, and dependable logistics — building lasting commercial relationships our clients rely on.
